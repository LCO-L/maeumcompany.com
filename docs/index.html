MAEUM · A public-interest project

On-device AI should belong
to everyone.

MAEUM works to keep on-device AI within reach of the people who need it most — especially those the cloud has left behind.

What we stand for Partner with us

Cloud AI runs on someone else's computer.
On-device AI runs on yours.

What we believe

That difference is not abstract. It is a family's data staying in the home. A hospital's records never leaving the ward. A country running its own AI on hardware it already owns — nothing rented, nothing sent abroad. When the AI runs on the device, the person keeps control.

Who this is for

AI that reaches the last mile.

Developing nations

AI that runs on the hardware a country already owns — no data sent abroad, nothing rented, no permission needed.

Communities offline

Where the network is thin or absent, it still works — fully on the device, in the local language, for the clinic and the classroom alike.

Public & multilateral partners

For those who work for the public good — ministries, development banks, foundations, and the agencies behind them.

Our principles

Three things we hold to.

1

People succeed first

Our job is to help people succeed. When growth and that duty pull apart, the person comes first — every time.

2

On-device by default

Your data, your computing, your decisions — kept in your own hands. Running on the device is the starting point, not an add-on.

3

No gatekeepers

No middleman between a person and their tools. We take the gate away — we don't become one.

How this is set up

Why the mission sits apart from the company.

MAEUM's technology is built with an operating company, maeum.io. But the name MAEUM and the promise behind it are kept apart from it — so that investment can grow the business without buying the direction. Money can shape the company. It can't buy what MAEUM is for.

Money comes and goes. What we're for doesn't.

MAEUM is early. This page says plainly what we're for — and invites the people who share it.

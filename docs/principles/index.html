Our principles

What we stand for.

A short list. It says what MAEUM works by, and holds us to it — through every round of funding, through every change of season.

We start from one idea: when there's only one road, people end up serving it; when there are many, they stay free. AI — the most powerful tool of our time — shouldn't become one more road that only a few can travel. These principles are how we try to keep that from happening.

People succeed first

Our job is to help people succeed — not to grow at any cost, not to win at any cost, but to leave the person in front of us better off. When growth and that duty pull apart, the person comes first. Revenue is what follows from keeping this promise, never a reason to break it.

On-device by default

A person, a company, a country should hold their own data, their own computing, and their own decisions in their own hands. So we build AI that runs on the device — not in someone else's cloud, not on rented permission. This isn't a convenience we trade away. It's where we start.

No gatekeepers

Too many people were shut out — for lack of money, credentials, or connections — from tools that should have been theirs. We take the gate away; we don't become one. What we make should be affordable, understandable, and open to the person who used to be turned away.

Independence

MAEUM isn't tied to a single vendor or owned by a single interest. Our technology can run on many models from many makers, so no one supplier can hold our users hostage. And the mission is kept apart from the operating company — so the direction of MAEUM can be kept, but not bought.

Honest about where we are

We won't dress up ambition as achievement. MAEUM is early, and we say so plainly. These principles describe what we're for and what we intend — and where we haven't arrived yet, we'll name it honestly instead of claiming otherwise. Trust is the only standing worth having.

That's what MAEUM is for — and who it's for.
